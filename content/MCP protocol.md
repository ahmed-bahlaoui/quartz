The basic capabilities of a Generative AI model depend on its **pretraining details**, the **training data** and the **model architecture**. To make these pretrained models perform better and improve their relevance and coherence to your task, you must provide a good context to it.

Here context refers to the information the model uses to generate relevant and coherent responses.

Context determines how the model understands and continues a conversation, completes a text, or generates an image.

MCP is an open protocol that standardizes how your LLM applications connect to and work with your tools and data sources.

Secure connections between such systems are often protected by asymmetric cryptography like the [[RSA Algorithm|RSA algorithm]].

For more on the models these connect to, see [[Convolutional Neural Networks|neural networks]] and related notes in this garden. Live at [[My Digital Garden|home]].
